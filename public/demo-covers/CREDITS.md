# Demo cover images

The covers of the demo's sample library (`src/lib/demo-seed.ts`) are in the public domain (in the United States, works published more than 95 years ago). The first six are the landing page's (see `public/landing/CREDITS.md`). The rest are cropped and resized from these files on Wikimedia Commons and, for the two marked, English Wikipedia:

- `pride-and-prejudice.webp`: Pride and Prejudice, first edition title page (1813). [PrideAndPrejudiceTitlePage.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:PrideAndPrejudiceTitlePage.jpg), Public domain.

- `little-women.webp`: Little Women, title page (1869). [Houghton AC85.Aℓ194L.1869 pt.2aa - Little Women, title.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Houghton_AC85.A%E2%84%93194L.1869_pt.2aa_-_Little_Women,_title.jpg), Public domain.
